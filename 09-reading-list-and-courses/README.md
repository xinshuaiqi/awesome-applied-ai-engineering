# 09 · Reading List & Courses

> Signal-dense resources for applied AI engineers, ranked by production relevance rather than academic prestige.

---

## Books

### Essential
- **[AI Engineering — Chip Huyen](https://www.oreilly.com/library/view/ai-engineering/9781098166298/)** (2025) — Building LLM-powered products: context, RAG, agents, evaluation, deployment.
- **[Designing Machine Learning Systems — Chip Huyen](https://www.oreilly.com/library/view/designing-machine-learning/9781098107956/)** (2022) — System design for production ML.

### Architecture & Data
- **[Software Architecture: The Hard Parts — Ford et al.](https://www.oreilly.com/library/view/software-architecture-the/9781492086888/)** — Trade-off analysis for integrating AI into enterprise software.
- **[Fundamentals of Data Engineering — Reis & Housley](https://www.oreilly.com/library/view/fundamentals-of-data/9781098108298/)** — Data systems that feed AI pipelines.

### Understanding Models
- **[The Little Book of Deep Learning — François Fleuret](https://fleuret.org/public/lbdl.pdf)** — Free, dense, covers transformers well.
- **[Hands-On Large Language Models — Alammar & Grootendorst](https://www.oreilly.com/library/view/hands-on-large-language/9781098150952/)** — Visual, practical intuition.

## Courses

### Applied & Production
- **[Full Stack LLM Bootcamp](https://fullstackdeeplearning.com/llm-bootcamp/)** — Free; RAG, agents, deployment, evals.
- **[LangChain Academy](https://academy.langchain.com)** — Free; LangGraph from intro to advanced agent patterns.
- **[DeepLearning.AI Short Courses](https://www.deeplearning.ai/short-courses/)** — Free; prompt engineering, RAG, agents, evals with vendor partners.
- **[Fast.ai Practical Deep Learning](https://course.fast.ai)** — Free; code-first.

### Foundations
- **[Karpathy — Neural Networks: Zero to Hero](https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PLAqhIrjkxbuWI23v9cThsA9GvCAUhRvKZ)** — Build GPT from scratch.
- **[Stanford CS224N](https://web.stanford.edu/class/cs224n/)** — NLP with deep learning.
- **[MIT 6.S191](http://introtodeeplearning.com)** — Intro to deep learning.

## Design Guides from Model Providers

- **[Building Effective Agents — Anthropic](https://www.anthropic.com/research/building-effective-agents)** — Workflow vs agent patterns; source of the pattern catalog in chapter 03.
- **[Anthropic Cookbook](https://github.com/anthropics/anthropic-cookbook)** — Practical Claude examples.
- **[OpenAI Cookbook](https://github.com/openai/openai-cookbook)** — Production-ready examples and prompt templates.
- **[Prompt Engineering Guide (dair-ai)](https://github.com/dair-ai/Prompt-Engineering-Guide)** — Techniques, security, evaluation.

---

## Enterprise Adoption & Value (Non-Technical Reading)

- **Deloitte — State of AI in the Enterprise** — Annual survey on adoption, ROI, governance, and skills.
- **IBM — AI adoption challenges** — Data readiness, integration, skills, governance barriers.
- **McKinsey — State of AI** — Annual survey on enterprise AI value capture.

Read these alongside the technical chapters: most scaling failures are data, integration, governance, and change-management problems.
